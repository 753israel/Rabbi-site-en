Helping Large Families

We support families with love and provide the care they need to live with dignity and joy.

Shopping Vouchers for Families in Need — A Light of Kindness in the Community

We distributed shopping vouchers to families in need so they could buy groceries with dignity and bring joy to their homes. Each voucher is more than financial help: it is an embrace, support, and the feeling that someone cares.
