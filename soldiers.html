Work with IDF Soldiers

Encouraging service members and giving them warmth, support, and a victorious Jewish spirit.

A Mitzvah of Encouragement and Support for IDF Soldiers

Through an activity of kindness and encouragement, I met a soldier and strengthened him in a moment of fellowship and mutual responsibility. I gave him a kind word, a smile, and practical support—an act that brings light and strength to those who protect the Jewish people.

The Mitzvah of Tefillin with IDF Soldiers in the Field

During a field visit, I paused for a moment of holiness and helped soldiers put on tefillin. It was a moment of mitzvah, connection, and unity that strengthens the heart and brings light to those protecting the Jewish people.

Dear Soldier — A Mitzvah of Encouragement and Giving in the Field

As part of my work of kindness, I went into the field to encourage a devoted soldier protecting the Jewish people. I brought him a fresh baguette and a cold drink to give him strength to continue at his post.

Encouraging a Soldier in the Field — A Mitzvah of a Big Heart

While encouraging IDF soldiers, I met a soldier standing guard with devotion and humility. I stopped beside him and offered a kind word, a smile, and support—a small moment of Jewish connection that gives strength to those who protect us day and night.

A Mitzvah of Encouragement — A Moment of Light with a Soldier in the Field

During my work of encouragement and kindness, I met a devoted soldier at his post. A kind word, smile, and support can add strength to someone protecting the Jewish people. One good deed can light an entire day.

A Mitzvah of Light — Giving Candles and Oil to a Soldier in the Field

I met a soldier and gave him candles and oil to light a hanukkiah. This small moment of light amid the challenges of service joined mitzvah, fellowship, and spiritual encouragement.

Bringing the Light of Sukkot to a Soldier — Blessing the Four Species in the Field

I met a soldier and gave him the Four Species so he could make the blessing properly in the field. This moment of holiness and joy during service connects mitzvah, fellowship, and the light of Sukkot.

A Moment of Holiness in the Field — Soldiers Bless the Four Species

I met a group of soldiers in the field and gave them the opportunity to bless the Four Species. Seeing them smile, hold the lulav and etrog, and bless wholeheartedly was a soul-filling moment.

Distributing Chumash, Psalms, Tanya, and Charity Boxes — Spiritual Protection on the Road

I distributed Chumash, Psalms, Tanya, and charity boxes to soldiers, as encouraged by the Lubavitcher Rebbe. Seeing the soldiers receive them with smiles and excitement was heartwarming; these gifts add light, protection, and blessing.

Distributing Food and Encouragement — The Chabad Mitzvah Tank in the Field

Through the Chabad mitzvah tank, I went into the field to encourage soldiers serving day and night. I distributed hot food, smiles, and kind words—small acts of kindness that give strength to those protecting the Jewish people.

Children's Drawings for Soldiers — Words of Thanks that Strengthen the Heart

I distributed moving drawings that children made especially for soldiers. Each drawing is full of love, innocence, and gratitude—a small message that gives soldiers great strength and reminds them that the Jewish people stand behind them.

A Mitzvah in a Tent — Putting on Tefillin and Giving Gifts to Soldiers in the Field

In a field tent, amid the tasks and pressure, we put on tefillin together and distributed gifts of holiness and encouragement. It was a moment of mitzvah, fellowship, and living Judaism that reminded soldiers they are surrounded by love, prayers, and support.

Children's Drawings from the United States — Support from the World for IDF Soldiers

I brought soldiers moving drawings made especially for them by children in the United States. Each drawing is full of love, innocence, and prayers for their well-being, showing soldiers that they are surrounded by warmth and support from around the world.

A Mitzvah in a Tent — Putting on Tefillin and Encouraging Soldiers in the Field

In a field tent, we paused with soldiers for a moment of holiness and connection. We put on tefillin together, shared words of encouragement, and brought gifts that add light and protection.

Encouraging Police Officers — Chumash, Psalms, Tanya, and Charity Boxes for Protection and Blessing

I met devoted police officers and gave them Chumash, Psalms, Tanya, and charity boxes—items that add protection, blessing, and spiritual strength. Their smiles and excitement were a heartening reminder of the importance of bringing good into their daily work.

Reaching the Outposts — Putting on Tefillin and Bringing Gifts to Soldiers

As part of my work to encourage soldiers, I also enter the outposts to meet fighters closely. Between operational duties and pressure, we put on tefillin together, distribute gifts of holiness and encouragement, and offer a moment of light and joy.
