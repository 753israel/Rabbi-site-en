Helping the Community

Acts of kindness for the community and its cherished families

Thousands of Hanukkiahs for the Community — Lighting Unity and Kindness

In a moving, light-filled activity, thousands of hanukkiahs were distributed to community families so every home could light Hanukkah candles with dignity, joy, and hope. Each hanukkiah is more than a gift: it is a spark of Jewish light, tradition, and connection. This distribution strengthens families, unites the public, and reminds us how much power a small light has to dispel great darkness.

Lag BaOmer Parade — Thousands of Children and Families in Light, Joy, and Prizes

On a day full of light and joy, thousands of children, teens, and families joined a beautiful, unifying Lag BaOmer parade. The streets filled with singing, flags, smiles, and an atmosphere of holiness and connection. Prizes and gifts added special excitement for everyone. It was a moment of community unity, living Jewish tradition, and pure joy that strengthens the bond between generations.

Bringing Light and Encouragement — Hospital Visits and Tefillin

Community volunteers visit Jews in hospitals, encourage them, and help them put on tefillin. A smile, a kind word, and tefillin become a great light during difficult days. These visits offer strength, hope, and human warmth, reminding every Jew that they are not alone.

Handmade Matzah for the Community — Bringing Freedom, Light, and Blessing to Every Home

Before Passover, handmade matzah was distributed to community families so every home could celebrate the Festival of Freedom with dignity and joy. Every box is more than a mitzvah: it is living tradition, a connection between generations, and a gift of Jewish light.

Putting on Tefillin in the Community — Strengthening Hearts and Lighting Souls

Community volunteers go out each day to help Jews of all ages put on tefillin—at home, on the street, at work, and wherever someone seeks encouragement. One moment with tefillin becomes a meeting of warmth, connection, identity, and light.

Putting on Tefillin with Youth — Strengthening a Generation of Faith and Jewish Pride

Volunteers help young people put on tefillin, giving them a moment of holiness, meaning, and Jewish pride. With a smile, a kind word, and tefillin, these meetings open hearts and strengthen identity and connection to tradition.

Distributing Food to Families — Bringing Warmth, Dignity, and Abundance to the Community

Community volunteers distribute food to families in need. Every bag, package, and product is more than food: it is a message of care, human warmth, and support when it is needed most. The distribution strengthens the community and gives families dignity and hope.

Distributing Snacks to Kindergarten Children — A Small Joy with a Big Heart

As part of the organization's community work, we brought tasty surprise snacks to kindergarten children. Every small snack is more than a treat: it is a gesture of love, attention, and a sincere wish to brighten the children's day.

Prize Distribution at the Lag BaOmer Parade — Joy, Unity, and Light for Community Children

At the special Lag BaOmer parade, hundreds of boys and girls received wonderful prizes that added excitement and light to the event. The prizes expressed love, investment, and a real desire to empower the younger generation.

Community Farbrengen — Moments of Unity, Warmth, and Spiritual Strength

Residents, families, and friends gathered for a special, inspiring farbrengen of connection, joy, and encouragement. Around a generously set table, uplifting melodies, Chassidic stories, and words of encouragement warmed hearts and created deep unity.

Distributing Hanukkah Candles to Businesses — Lighting the Community through Mitzvot

We distributed Hanukkah candles to business owners throughout the city. Each candle is a small light that connects people, strengthens the spirit of the holiday, and spreads joy. The visits created moments of warmth, smiles, and Jewish pride.

Torah Classes with Generous Hospitality — Nourishing the Body and Lighting the Soul

Special Torah classes are held with generous food and drink. People of all ages gather around a well-set table to hear Torah teachings, learn, ask questions, and grow closer. Both body and soul are nourished with light, inspiration, and authentic Jewish joy.

One Sukkah — Uniting Hearts and Blessing the Community

We opened the sukkah to everyone who wished to enter, make a blessing, eat, drink, and feel at home. People of every age blessed the Four Species, sat together, and enjoyed a warm, unifying atmosphere.

Handmade Matzah for Women and Girls — Bringing Passover Light to Every Home

We distributed handmade matzah to women and girls so that each one could celebrate Passover with connection, joy, and living Jewish tradition. This distribution is far more than a gift: it is a bridge of love, unity, and continuity between generations.

On the Court Too — Encouraging Youth and Putting on Tefillin with Love

During basketball games and every youth activity, we are there to encourage and offer tefillin to anyone who wants to connect. These simple moments become opportunities for holiness, Jewish identity, and a deep connection to the soul.

Spreading Hanukkah Light — Even During COVID, Every Office Received Light and Joy

During the COVID period, despite distance and challenges, we continued spreading Hanukkah light everywhere. We visited offices, businesses, and workplaces with candles, hanukkiahs, and oil so everyone could light a small light that dispels great darkness.
