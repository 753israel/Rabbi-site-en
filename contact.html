Join Our Work

Together, we can spread the light of kindness and change the lives of families, soldiers, and children in the community.

Support for Families

  • Food basket for a family: $230 per family (dozens of families benefit every week).
  • Supermarket vouchers totaling $5,000 are distributed each week.

Flagship Project: Building a Home of Kindness

Together, let us build our home—a global center of kindness in the holy city of Safed (project cost: $5.5 million).

The center will include a synagogue, event hall, large kitchen, mikveh, and extensive children's activities.

Jewish Holidays and Festivals

We make sure every family can celebrate with dignity and joy. Through the holiday project, we invest approximately $100,000 for each holiday.

  • Financial grants distributed according to household size.
  • Minimum assistance of $500 per family to meet holiday needs.

Support for Orphans and Widows

We support widows and orphans with ongoing warmth, security, and care:

  • Food assistance and substantial shopping vouchers.
  • Purchasing clothing and needed supplies, with financial support in times of crisis.
  • Essential help with bills: electricity, municipal tax, and water.

Activities for IDF Soldiers (annual cost: $100,000)

  • Barbecue evenings and distribution of tefillin and prayer shawls.
  • Distribution of tens of thousands of Psalms books and Tanya books, and the dedication of a Torah scroll for the soldiers.
